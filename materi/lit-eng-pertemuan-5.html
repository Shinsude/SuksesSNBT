Literasi Inggris · Meeting 5

Tone & Purpose

Mengenali tone (nada penulis) dan purpose (tujuan penulisan) berdasarkan pilihan kata dan cara penyampaian.

📘 Penjelasan Materi

Tone = sikap/nada penulis terhadap pembaca atau topik (mis. optimistic, critical, neutral, humorous, persuasive).

Purpose = tujuan penulis menulis teks (mis. to inform, to persuade, to entertain, to explain, to warn).

🧩 Common Tones to Recognize
  • Neutral/Objectively – hanya menyajikan fakta, tanpa penilaian.
  • Positive/Optimistic – menunjukkan harapan/pujian.
  • Critical/Concerned – mengkritik atau menunjukkan kekhawatiran.
  • Persuasive – berusaha meyakinkan pembaca.
  • Amused/Humorous – sedikit bercanda atau menghibur.
🪜 Strategy
  1. Look at word choice. Kata bermuatan emosi biasanya jadi petunjuk tone.
  2. See the overall goal. Apakah penulis cuma menjelaskan, atau ingin mengajak/menyakinkan?
  3. Focus on the main idea. Kadang tone & purpose bisa disimpulkan dari ide utama paragraf.
  4. Eliminate extremes. Hindari opsi yang terlalu kuat kalau teksnya netral.

✍️ Contoh Soal & Pembahasan

📝 Soal

"Cycling is one of the simplest ways to stay fit. It improves heart health, builds stamina, and is gentle on the joints. Best of all, it produces no air pollution."

What is the author's purpose in writing the passage?

  • A. To criticize car drivers
  • B. To inform readers about the benefits of cycling
  • C. To describe how to fix a bicycle
  • D. To persuade people to ban cars
💡 Pembahasan
  1. Look at content: The text lists benefits (heart health, stamina, joints, no pollution).
  2. Tone: Positive and informative, not harsh or attacking.
  3. Purpose: Simply gives information about benefits → to inform.
  4. Why not others: No criticism of drivers (A), no instructions (C), and it's not strongly pushing to ban cars (D).
Answer: B
⚠️ Principle: If the text mainly lists facts/benefits without urging action, the purpose is usually "to inform".

🧠 Contoh Soal: Tone

📝 Soal

"It is worrying that so much plastic waste ends up in our oceans. Marine animals often mistake it for food, with deadly results. Stronger rules are clearly needed to stop this before it's too late."

Which best describes the author's tone?

  • A. Humorous and playful
  • B. Concerned and urgent
  • C. Indifferent and casual
  • D. Sarcastic and mocking
💡 Pembahasan
  1. Key words: "worrying", "deadly results", "before it's too late" show strong concern.
  2. Tone: Worried and urging action → concerned and urgent.
  3. Eliminate: A,C,D are opposite of the serious/emotional tone shown.
Answer: B
⚠️ Principle: Words like "worrying", "danger", "too late" almost always signal a concerned/urgent tone.

🏋️ Latihan Mandiri

🎯 Soal Latihan

"If we want cleaner air in our cities, we must use public transport more often. Small changes by many people can make a big difference."

What is the author's tone?

  • A. Angry and bitter
  • B. Persuasive and encouraging
  • C. Bored and uninterested
  • D. Cruel and harsh
🔑 Pembahasan Singkat
  1. Look at verb: "we must..." shows a call to action.
  2. Encouraging words: "can make a big difference" gives hope.
  3. Answer: B (persuasive and encouraging).
Answer: B

📌 Rangkuman

🎯 Key Points
  • Tone = attitude (how the writer feels). Look at word choice.
  • Purpose = goal (why written). Ask: "What does the writer want to do?"
  • Common purposes: to inform, to persuade, to entertain, to warn, to explain.
  • Common tones: neutral, critical, concerned, optimistic, humorous, persuasive.
  • Tip: If only facts & benefits → to inform. If urging/recommending → often to persuade.
