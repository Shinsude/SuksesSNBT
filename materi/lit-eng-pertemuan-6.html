Literasi Inggris · Meeting 6

Evaluasi Akhir

Mixed practice: Main Idea, Vocabulary in Context, Inference, and Tone & Purpose.

🎯 Goal

What to review
  • Main idea vs detail
  • Vocabulary in context
  • Inference (read between the lines)
  • Tone & purpose

✍️ Mixed Practice

Question 1

"Reading for just a few minutes a day can greatly improve vocabulary and focus. Over time, it also helps reduce stress by giving the mind a break from screens."

What is the main idea of the passage?

  • A. Screens are bad for everyone
  • B. Reading daily has many mental benefits
  • C. Stress only comes from work
  • D. Vocabulary is hard to improve
Explanation 1

The passage mentions vocabulary, focus, and stress reduction. B covers all of them.

Answer: B
Question 2

"The old man walked slowly across the street, leaning heavily on his cane. A group of students noticed him and quietly helped him to the other side without being asked."

What can be inferred from the passage?

  • A. The students were in a hurry
  • B. The students showed kindness and concern
  • C. The old man refused help
  • D. The cane was broken
Explanation 2

Helping without being asked shows care. B is the correct inference.

Answer: B
Question 3

"Plastic waste is choking our oceans. It is time for us to cut down on single-use plastics before the damage becomes irreversible."

What is the author's tone?

  • A. Playful and joking
  • B. Concerned and urgent
  • C. Calm and indifferent
  • D. Bored and unbothered
Explanation 3

"choking" and "before... becomes irreversible" show strong concern and urgency. B.

Answer: B

💡 Quick Tips

Final reminders
  • Main idea: Covers the whole passage, not just one example.
  • Inference: Must be supported by text. Avoid outside knowledge.
  • Vocabulary: Use context clues + "replace the word" trick.
  • Tone/Purpose: Watch for strong/emotional words and calls to action.
  • Eliminate: Too extreme, too narrow, or not stated.
